Core platform capabilities

Plan enrollment & coverage

Browse Silver, Gold, and Platinum tier health insurance plans. Review detailed deductibles, annual benefit limits, room rate ceilings, and copayment structures before applying directly online.

Hospital desk & eligibility

Hospital administrative officers can query patient policy numbers directly at admission, verify coverage validity and remaining limits, and prepare streamlined settlement requests.

Claims adjudication & tracking

Submit reimbursement claims with itemized medical invoices and diagnosis summaries. Dedicated claims officers review documentation, verify policy terms, and record auditable adjudication decisions.

Premium billing & receipts

Complete monthly and annual premium payments with credit card, bank transfer, or mobile wallet options. Access verifiable PDF payment receipts and submit refund requests when applicable.
